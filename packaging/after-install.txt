Pixel Slides is installed.

Your decks live in Documents\Pixel Slides\decks. The sample decks are copied
there the first time the app starts (after an update, only new ones are added).

- Press "Edit" next to a deck to open it in your text editor. Save the file
  and the open slides reload on their own.
- "New deck" starts from Basic, Talk or Lesson. "Duplicate" makes a copy.
- "Export PDF" and "Handout PDF" (slides with speaker notes) save to
  Documents\Pixel Slides\exports, using Microsoft Edge in the background
  (nothing extra to download).
- In a deck, press ? for every keyboard shortcut.

Uninstalling keeps your decks.
